# NovaRetail Internal IT Support Handbook

## VPN Access
Employees working outside the office must use the company VPN before accessing internal systems. Install the NovaSecure VPN client from the internal Software Portal. Sign in with your company email and password, complete MFA, and select the closest corporate gateway. If the connection fails, first confirm internet connectivity, restart the client, and try another corporate gateway. Do not disable endpoint security or firewall settings to make the VPN work. If the problem remains, open an IT ticket with a screenshot of the error.

## Password Reset Policy
Company passwords must be at least 14 characters. Employees should use the self-service identity portal for normal password resets. After five failed login attempts, the account may be temporarily locked. If self-service recovery fails, contact IT Service Desk. Employees must never share passwords or MFA codes with support personnel.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN, cloud administration, and other protected company systems. The approved methods are the company authenticator app and registered hardware security keys. SMS should only be used when explicitly enabled as a recovery method.

## Software Installation
Employees should install business software only from the company Software Portal. Requests for software not listed in the portal require manager approval and IT security review. Administrator access is not granted simply to install unapproved applications.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop
Immediately report a lost or stolen company laptop to the IT Service Desk and your manager. Provide the device asset tag if available and the last known location. IT will initiate device lock/wipe procedures and coordinate with Security. Do not attempt to remotely recover the device yourself.

## Wi-Fi & Office Network Access
When in company offices, connect only to the secured Corp-Secure wireless network using your corporate credentials and installed machine certificates. Guest networks are reserved for external visitors and do not grant access to internal resources or printers. Do not set up personal mobile hotspots or connect unauthorized network hardware, such as personal routers or switches, to corporate network jacks.

## Data Storage & File Sharing
Store all business files and project documentation on approved cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive) rather than your local hard drive. Sharing files with external parties requires using secure, expiring share links with recipient verification enabled. The use of personal cloud drives, unauthorized third-party file transfer tools, or unencrypted USB thumb drives is strictly prohibited.

## Phishing & Suspicious Communications
Always verify sender addresses, links, and unexpected attachments before clicking or entering credentials. If you receive a suspicious email, do not forward, reply, or attempt to investigate it yourself—use the Report Phishing button directly inside your email client. If you suspect you have already clicked a malicious link or entered company credentials, notify the IT Service Desk and Security Operations immediately.

## Operating System & Application Updates
Keep your workstation updated by allowing scheduled system restarts when prompted. Critical security patches are deployed automatically and cannot be permanently postponed. Save your work and restart your machine at least once a week to ensure all updates, certificate renewals, and configuration profiles apply correctly.

## Hardware Support & Peripherals
Only company-issued or IT-approved peripherals (such as monitors, docking stations, headsets, and webcams) may be connected to your workstation. If you experience hardware failure, battery degradation, or physical damage, file a hardware replacement ticket through the IT Service Portal. Damaged or obsolete equipment must be returned to IT for proper e-waste disposal and asset tracking.

## Secure Remote Work Environment
When working remotely or in public spaces (e.g., coffee shops, airports), use a privacy screen filter if handling sensitive or customer data. Lock your screen (Win + L on Windows, Cmd + Ctrl + Q on macOS) whenever stepping away from your desk, even for a short period. Family members and guests must never be permitted to use company-issued hardware under any circumstance.
